My story

15 years of cakes

For the last 15 years I’ve been creating cakes for every occasion imaginable, from birthday cupcakes to multiple-tiered wedding cakes.

If you have a special day coming up and would like something special to mark the occasion, pop me a message. I’d love to help!

As well as cakes I make brownies, blondies, fudge, cupcakes, flapjacks, traybakes and cookies, with dairy, gluten and nut-free and vegan options too. And on Instagram, I share my Persian bakes as @saras_persian_bakes.
