Common questions

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can. Popular dates book up, especially for weddings and tiered cakes. For something soon, message on WhatsApp and Sarah will let you know if she can fit you in.

How much will my cake cost?

Every cake is made to order, so the price depends on size and design. Send your idea and you’ll get a quote before anything is confirmed.

What sizes do you do?

Celebration cakes come in sizes like 8", 9", 10" and 12", right up to multi-tiered wedding cakes. Not sure? Tell Sarah how many people you’re feeding.

Do you do dairy-free, gluten-free, nut-free or vegan?

Yes, dairy, gluten and nut-free and vegan options are available. Please mention any allergies when you order.

Do you deliver?

Cakes are collected by arrangement. Ask Sarah about delivery when you order.
